GPU init-container chicken-and-egg: when a k8s Job has initContainer (scale-down) + main container (GPU trainer) in the same pod, the pod cannot schedule until the GPU is free — but the init container that would FREE the GPU never runs because the pod is stuck Pending. Fix: either (a) submit a separate one-shot scale-down Job BEFORE the training Job, (b) always manually `kubectl scale deployment sm0l --replicas=0 -n b00t-inference` before `just finetune-train-kube-smol`, or (c) wire a pre-job hook. Pattern: NEVER rely on an init container to free a resource that the same pod needs to acquire to schedule.
