reserved claim names must stay spec-shaped: decode() always re-parses your claims a 2nd time into an internal struct assuming sub/iss/aud are strings and exp/nbf are numbers, even when Validation disables those checks (required_spec_claims cleared, validate_exp=false). If your own claims type reuses one of those 5 field names with a different shape (e.g. sub as a nested JSON object/enum), decode() throws a raw JSON parse error instead of a clean type-mismatch, because the shape mismatch desyncs the parser mid-object rather than failing cleanly on one field. Fix: #[serde(rename = "other_name")] on the colliding field to change its wire key while keeping whatever Rust field name you want. Applies to jsonwebtoken >=9, confirmed on 10.4.0.
