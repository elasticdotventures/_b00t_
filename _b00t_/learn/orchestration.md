Parallel Claude Code sub-agents (isolation: worktree, run_in_background) exhaust session token/credit budget fast when fanned out across many issues at once (7 of 9 agents hit session rate limits simultaneously in one sprint attempt). Fix: cap concurrency to 2-3 agents at a time, monitor budget between batches, and prefer direct orchestrator-driven work (Bash/b00t-cli) over spawning a frontier-model sub-agent for routine verify/fix/commit work -- it is both cheaper and, in practice, higher quality (the orchestrator caught 4 real main-branch bugs the sub-agents could not self-diagnose). For remaining routine implement/fix work, prefer local ch0nky-tier compute (opencode ACP server on local Qwen3.6/DeepSeek via the hive) over additional Claude sub-agents, since local tier does not consume Claude API credit.
