⌨️ HOW TO INTERACT:

  Navigate:
    ↓ (down arrow) or j → Move to next option
    ↑ (up arrow)   or k → Move to previous option
  
  Filter:
    Type text instantly filters options
    Examples: "status", "joke", "deploy", "test"
    Clear filter: Backspace or Ctrl+U
  
  Select/Exit:
    Enter → Execute selected option
    ESC   → Cancel and close menu
    Tab   → Jump to next match (if filtering)
